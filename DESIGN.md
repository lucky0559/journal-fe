# Journal: design system

## Theme: Lamplight
Scene: someone in bed or on the sofa around 10pm, one lamp on, the phone dimmed, writing a few honest lines they won't
show anyone. That calls for a dark, low-glare surface with warm light on it, not a bright cream page. The app is dark
only for now (no system-driven light theme yet).

Colour strategy: **Restrained** for working screens (tinted neutral plus one action colour), **Committed** on the
welcome and sign-in screens, where the three brand shapes carry the page. The mood scale is the one place the full
palette appears.

## Colour (source of truth: `mobile/src/theme/tokens.ts`)
Designed in OKLCH, shipped as hex.

| Role | Token | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Page | `colors.bg` | `#081a1c` | Deep spruce ink, tinted toward hue 205 (never pure black) |
| Panel | `colors.surface` | `#112527` | Check-in card, tab bar, sheets |
| Words | `colors.text` | `#f1ebdc` | Warm paper cream, 15:1 on the page |
| Action | `colors.accent` | `#eca338` | Lamp amber: primary buttons, selection, links. One per screen |
| Calm | `colors.accent2` | `#5fb7a5` | Tide teal: friends, selected chips, switches |
| Error | `colors.danger` | `#f69186` | Errors and destructive actions only |
| Mood low | `colors.moodLow` | `#7a77ba` | Dusk violet |
| Mood high | `colors.moodHigh` | `#f8ac3d` | Lamp amber. In between, the scale passes through rose |
| Photo border | `colors.paper` | `#faf7ee` | Printed photos stay light on the dark page |

Ramps (`ramp.accent`, `ramp.accent2`, `ramp.neutral`) run by **emphasis, not by light to dark**: step 100 is the quietest
surface, 900 the loudest ink. `neutral[100]` is a raised card, `neutral[700]` is secondary text, `accent[200]` is a tinted
panel and `accent[900]` is the ink that sits on it. Use them as pairs; `src/theme/contrast.test.ts` holds every pair the
app uses to at least 4.5:1.

Rules
- Never write a hex or `rgba(...)` colour outside `src/theme` (a test enforces hex).
- Never leave a `Text` without a colour: `heading()` and `body()` carry `colors.text` so nothing falls back to black.
- Amber means "go". Don't use it for errors; use `colors.danger`.
- Mood colours always come from `moodColor()` / `MOOD_COLORS`, which mix in OKLCH.

## Type
Caprasimo for headings and the date, Figtree for everything else. Reading text is 18px on a 32px line.

## Motion
150 to 300 ms, ease-out. Motion shows a change of state: the tab highlight slides, the page slides in from the side it
was turned from, progress dots stretch. No decorative loops.

## Identity
Three shapes from the welcome screen: dusk violet circle, tide teal blob, lamp amber dot. They are the app icon, the
splash mark and the welcome illustration (`mobile/assets/*`, generated by a script from the same shapes).
