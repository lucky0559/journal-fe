# Journal: product context

register: product

## Users
Young adults who are new to journaling. They write for a minute or two, usually at the end of the day, on their phone,
often in bed or on the sofa with the lights low. They are not "productive journalers"; a blank page frightens them.

## Purpose
A low-pressure place to put a day down: a mood, a few words, maybe a photo or a voice note. The whole journal follows
the person to any phone. Pages are private by default; sharing with friends or publicly is a choice made per page, and
mood and feelings are never visible to anyone but the author.

## Tone
Warm, quiet, unhurried. Plain words. Nothing that scores, streaks or nags. One gentle nightly nudge, never more.

## Anti-references
- Productivity and habit trackers (streak counters, progress rings, gamified badges).
- Clinical mood or therapy apps (charts first, feelings second).
- Social feeds that reward posting (counts, trending, follower numbers).

## Principles
1. The page comes first. Controls stay out of the way until needed.
2. Private by default, honest about where things are stored.
3. Mood is colour, not a number: the scale runs from dusk violet to lamp amber everywhere it appears.
4. Calm motion that explains a change of state, never decoration.
