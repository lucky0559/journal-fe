# Journ.al: Google Play release notes

Play Console "What's new" accepts up to 500 characters per language. Paste the text between the tags for the language.

## 1.0.0 (versionCode 1): first release

```
<en-US>
Welcome to Journ.al, a quiet place to put your day down.

• Pick a colour for how you feel, no numbers or scores
• Write a few words, add photos, voice notes or stickers
• Watch your month fill with colour in the calendar
• Pages are private by default; share with friends or the public only when you choose
• Lock with a PIN or fingerprint
• One gentle reminder a night, never more
• Your journal follows you to any phone
</en-US>
```

(About 430 characters.)

## 1.0.1 (versionCode 2)

```
<en-US>
A smoother first run and a few fixes.

• New: a light theme. Choose Dark or Light in Me
• The keyboard's check key now continues on the name screen, and a failed save no longer leaves you stuck
• Pop-ups such as Add a friend sit above the keyboard, and the Back button closes them
• Fewer permissions requested
</en-US>
```

## 1.0.2 (versionCode 3)

```
<en-US>
Open your friends' pages.

• Tap a friend's page, in the feed or on their profile, to read it in full, with photos, voice notes and reactions
• A new Your friends list in the Friends tab
</en-US>
```

## 1.0.4 (versionCode 5)

(1.0.3, versionCode 4, was uploaded to Play first, without notifications, report/block or the Lumi loader; this release includes everything below.)

```
<en-US>
A fresh look.

• Meet Lumi, your journal's little companion, now also your loading spinner
• Today, Timeline and Me are now soft tiles, with a floating tab bar
• Pages and numbers ease into place (and stay still if you prefer less motion)
• This month at a glance: your days as a grid of mood colours
• Notifications for friend requests and reactions, with a bell on Today
• Report or block anyone from their page; manage blocks in Me
</en-US>
```

## 1.0.5 (versionCode 6)

```
<en-US>
Photos and refreshing.

• Tap any photo to see it full screen, on a soft blur of its own colours
• Pull down to refresh Today, Timeline, Me, your friends' pages, profiles and Notifications
</en-US>
```

## 1.0.6 (versionCode 7)

```
<en-US>
Stickers and all your pages in one place.
```
