# Journ.al: Google Play release notes

Play Console "What's new" accepts up to 500 characters per language. Paste the text between the tags for the language.

## 1.0.0 (versionCode 1): first release

```
<en-US>
Welcome to Journ.al, a quiet place to put your day down.

• Pick a colour for how you feel, no numbers or scores
• Write a few words, add photos, voice notes or stickers
• Watch your month fill with colour in the calendar
• Pages are private by default; share with friends or the public only when you choose
• Lock with a PIN or fingerprint
• One gentle reminder a night, never more
• Your journal follows you to any phone
</en-US>
```

(About 430 characters.)
