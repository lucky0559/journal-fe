## App content forms

**Privacy policy:** paste the URL above.

**App access (Sign in details):** "All or some functionality is restricted." The app needs a Google account to sign in.
Create a separate Google account just for the reviewers (for example a new Gmail used only for this), turn **2-Step Verification off** on it so the reviewer can sign in, and enter its email and password in the form. Do not use your own account. Instructions to give: "Tap Continue with Google and choose the account above. Onboarding asks for a name, a reminder time and an optional PIN (tap Not now). Then Today, Timeline, Friends and Me are reachable from the bottom bar."

**Ads:** No, the app does not contain ads.

**Content rating (IARC questionnaire):** category "Social networking / communication" is the closest fit.
- Violence, sexual content, profanity, drugs, gambling: No to all.
- Users can interact or share content with each other: **Yes** (friends and public pages; users can add friends and react).
- Shares user location: No.
- Digital purchases: No.
Expect a rating around Teen because users can share their own content. That is fine.

**Target audience:** **18 and over** only. Do not select under-13 or "appeals to children", which would pull in the Families policy.

**Data safety:**

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Does the app collect or share user data? | Yes, collects. Does not share with third parties (service providers acting for the app do not count as sharing). |
| Is all data encrypted in transit? | Yes (HTTPS) |
| Can users request deletion? | Yes. In app (Me, Delete my account) and at https://journ-al-be.vercel.app/delete-account |

Data types to tick, each: **Collected**, not shared, processed on our servers, purpose **App functionality** (and **Account management** for the first group):

- Personal info: **Name**, **Email address**, **User IDs** (Google account ID)
- Photos and videos: **Photos** (profile photo and photos the user adds; optional)
- Audio: **Voice or sound recordings** (voice notes; optional)
- App activity: **Other user-generated content** (journal text, stickers)
- Health and fitness: **Health info** (mood and feelings the user logs; the safe choice, ticked even though the app is not medical)

Not collected: location, financial info, contacts, messages, web browsing, app info and performance, device or other IDs, advertising ID.

**Government apps:** No.

**Financial features:** the app has none (select "My app doesn't provide any financial features").

**Health:** Journ.al logs a mood but gives no medical advice, diagnosis or treatment and is not a health product. If the form asks whether the app has health features, choose the option that says it does not provide any health features (or the nearest "other" option). If it lists a mood or journaling option, you can pick that instead. If you are unsure, send me a screenshot of the options.
